What this is

Not Just a Hackathon. The Payoff of a Whole Cycle.

This cycle didn't start today. It started weeks ago in the Sensemaking Sprint, where a room full of strangers mapped real problems, found the people who cared about the same ones, and built pods around them. Today, those pods show up as project teams — three to five people who spent the cycle turning a shared problem into something real.

This is where that work gets built. Not pitched, not planned — built. Teams spend the day with AI tools, research tools, and the same in-house tools The Labs builds for itself, and they leave with a working, deployed prototype and a live demo.
